## Final publishing shortlist

28 unique source assets are held for this production pass: 10 FIELD, 7 ROME/TRAVEL, 3 CURRENT/FOUNDER, 6 WORK, and 2 transition reserves. The live page deliberately uses the clear subset first; source candidates with privacy, people, or sensitive-background concerns stay in the manifest and are not silently published.

## Motion and spatial rules

- The real source remains a single flat image. “Spatial” in this pass means a low-amplitude CSS scale/translate effect, not a generated reconstruction.
- The selected Meta clip is video texture / editorial media, not an infinitely repeated background. The page plays it once in view, muted and with controls available.
- Reduced motion removes the drift and leaves still posters/content visible.
